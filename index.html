About the studio

Not just a class.
A dream studio.

The Dance Beat is Raebareli's Entertainment & Dream Studio — a home base for anyone chasing rhythm, strength or the spotlight. We run focused, small-batch training across dance, gymnastics and fitness, with private coaching and reality-show preparation for students ready to push further. Every session is built the same way we'd build a performance: with purpose, precision, and a beat that never stops.
